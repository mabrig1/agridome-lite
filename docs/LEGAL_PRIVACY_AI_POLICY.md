# AgriDome — Legal, Privacy and Responsible AI Implementation Policy
Version: draft 2026-10-16. Requires legal review before public publication.

## Applicable framework
- Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and NDPC General Application and Implementation Directive 2025: lawful basis, transparency, purpose limitation, minimisation, security, data-subject rights, processor governance and applicable cross-border safeguards.
- Nigeria's National AI Strategy and NITDA responsible AI initiatives inform good practice; do not describe drafts or strategies as binding AI legislation.
- Applicable consumer protection, electronic transactions, intellectual property and cybersecurity requirements must be assessed for the actual deployment and commercial model.

## Operational requirements
1. Publish a clear privacy notice identifying the actual legal controller, contact, processing purposes, lawful bases, recipients, international transfers, retention periods and complaint route. Do not invent controller identity or promise zero retention without vendor verification.
2. Require an affirmative, separately recorded decision before transmitting crop photographs to an external AI provider. Explain that images may contain identifiable people, locations and EXIF metadata. Remove EXIF and minimize image content before transfer. Refusal must not disable offline crop guidance.
3. Keep optional research participation separate from routine product use. Record consent version and withdrawal, protect pseudonymous codes, and prohibit re-identification of shared pilot data. Obtain research ethics and partner approvals before recruitment where applicable.
4. Provide accessible procedures for access, correction, deletion, objection, portability and complaints, subject to legal exceptions. Verify how local browser data and exported files are handled.
5. Perform a documented data protection impact/risk assessment before processing sensitive or large-scale farmer data. Review NDPC registration and reporting applicability with qualified counsel.
6. Maintain processor inventory and agreements covering AI providers, hosting, authentication, analytics and support. Verify international transfer arrangements and vendor retention.
7. Disclose AI limitations and uncertainty. Do not claim confirmed crop diagnosis, guaranteed yield, live market prices or validated accuracy without evidence. Escalate dangerous chemical and crop-health decisions to qualified professionals.
8. Apply access control, rate limits, secure secrets, audit logging without unnecessary personal data, incident response, and a tested deletion and backup process.
9. Publish accurate terms of use covering service availability, farmer responsibilities, intellectual property, dispute channels and liability limitations subject to applicable law.
10. Review any use by children, automated profiling, WhatsApp/SMS messaging, payment processing, or external data sharing before enabling those features.

## Release gate
No public legal-compliance badge until: controller details confirmed; notices and terms reviewed; image-consent workflow tested; vendor retention and transfers assessed; data-rights route functioning; security tests passing; and responsible person signs off.

## Evidence register
Maintain dated copies of: DPIA/risk review, processing inventory, consent text versions, agronomist validation, accessibility checks, security scan results, incident exercises and legal approvals.
