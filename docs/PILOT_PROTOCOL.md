# AgriDome Lite Farmer Pilot Protocol

## Protocol summary

**Working title:** Feasibility and preliminary outcomes of an offline, multilingual digital decision-support tool for smallholder horticulture in Enugu State, Nigeria.

**Version:** 1.0  
**Proposed duration:** 16 weeks  
**Initial sample:** 30 farmers  
**Primary purpose:** Establish usability, retention, data completeness and preliminary outcome estimates before a larger controlled evaluation.

This protocol is an operational starting point. A qualified research partner must review the design, participant information, consent language, data protection and any required ethics submission before recruitment begins.

## Research question

Can regular use of AgriDome Lite help smallholder horticulture farmers make better climate and pest-management decisions while generating sufficiently complete outcome data for a larger effectiveness study?

## Objectives

1. Determine whether farmers can use the app in low-connectivity environments.
2. Measure weekly retention and completeness of climate, pest and outcome records.
3. Estimate changes in harvest weight, pest-related loss, farm income and input cost relative to baseline.
4. Identify unsafe, unclear or impractical recommendations before wider deployment.
5. Document the implementation cost and support required per participating farmer.

## Study design

Use a prospective, single-arm feasibility pilot. Collect one baseline record before routine use, followed by one outcome record per farmer per week for 12 weeks. Reserve two weeks for recruitment/training and two weeks for close-out, interviews and analysis.

The pilot must not claim that observed changes were caused by AgriDome. Weather, crop cycle, market prices and other interventions may explain differences. A later study should use a comparison group or stepped-wedge design.

## Participants

### Proposed inclusion criteria

- Adult farmer able to provide informed consent.
- Currently growing at least one supported horticultural crop.
- Access to an Android phone personally or through a household/cooperative.
- Willing to record one short update each week.
- Farm located in the agreed pilot area.

### Proposed exclusion criteria

- No active crop during the observation period.
- Unable to understand the consent information in an available language.
- Participation would expose the person to material safety, privacy or economic risk.

Recruit through a farmer cooperative, extension office or university agriculture partner. Avoid recruiting only highly connected farmers; include women, young farmers and farmers with limited digital experience.

## Intervention

Participants receive:

- A 45-minute orientation and practice session.
- AgriDome Lite installed as a progressive web app.
- Access to climate logging, crop guidance, pest scanning, yield calculation and the AI advisor.
- A weekly reminder from the field coordinator.
- A named extension contact for serious crop-health or chemical-treatment decisions.

AgriDome is decision support, not a guaranteed diagnosis. AI outputs must not replace a qualified extension officer, pesticide label, regulator or emergency advice.

## Measures

### Feasibility outcomes

| Indicator | Definition | Initial success threshold |
|---|---|---:|
| Recruitment | Enrolled participants / eligible farmers approached | 60% |
| Activation | Enrolled farmers who complete baseline | 85% |
| Retention | Farmers submitting an outcome in week 12 | 70% |
| Data completeness | Required weekly fields completed | 80% |
| Active use | Median days used per week | 3 days |
| Safety review | Serious harmful recommendations confirmed | 0 |

### Preliminary outcome measures

- Weekly harvest in kilograms.
- Weekly sales income in Nigerian naira.
- Weekly farm costs in Nigerian naira.
- Estimated crop loss attributable to pests or disease.
- Number and category of pest-management actions.
- Time from noticing a crop problem to taking action.
- Farmer confidence and perceived usefulness at close-out.

Baseline and weekly data should use the same crop and cultivated area wherever possible. Record major changes such as replanting, crop failure, extreme weather or expansion.

## Data flow and protection

The current pilot module stores data locally on the participant's device. Nothing is automatically uploaded. Each participant receives a pseudonymous code. Name and phone are optional and must be removed before analysis unless follow-up requires them.

1. Participant reads the consent summary and opts in.
2. The app records consent version and timestamp.
3. Baseline and weekly records remain on-device.
4. The participant or field worker exports a CSV after confirming permission.
5. The research coordinator removes direct identifiers and stores the research dataset in an access-controlled location.
6. A separate, encrypted contact list is used only when necessary for follow-up.
7. Participants can withdraw and delete their on-device pilot data at any time.

Define retention periods, breach handling, authorized roles and the lawful basis for processing before recruitment. Follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act and partner-institution requirements.

## Analysis plan

Report feasibility indicators with counts and percentages. Summarize baseline and weekly measures with medians, ranges and simple plots. Compare each participant's later observations with their own baseline, while clearly labeling results exploratory.

Stratify descriptive results, where sample size permits, by crop, farm type, gender, age group and digital-experience level. Do not publish small cells that could identify participants.

## Operational roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Principal investigator | Scientific oversight, approvals and interpretation |
| Agriculture/extension lead | Agronomic validation and safety escalation |
| Field coordinator | Recruitment, consent, training and weekly follow-up |
| Data manager | De-identification, quality checks and secure storage |
| Product lead | App fixes, usage support and release control |
| Independent adviser | Reviews participant safety and complaints |

## Indicative 16-week schedule

| Weeks | Activity |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Partner onboarding, ethics/data review, field-worker training |
| 3–4 | Recruitment, consent, installation and baseline |
| 5–16 | Twelve weekly observations, support and safety review |
| 15–16 | Close-out interviews, cleaning, analysis and pilot report |

## Go/no-go decision

Proceed to a larger controlled study only if retention and completeness thresholds are met, no unresolved serious safety issue occurs, farmers report the advice is understandable, and the team can explain how the next study will handle comparison groups, seasonal effects and secure centralized data collection.
