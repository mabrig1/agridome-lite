# AgriDome Lite — Partner Positioning

## Africa's Offline Farm Command Center for Intensive Crop Production

**Observe. Decide. Act. Prove. — Even Offline.**

AgriDome Lite is an open-source, mobile-first farm intelligence platform designed for African smallholder greenhouse and intensive-vegetable farmers and the programmes that support them.

### The problem

Most digital agriculture products solve one part of the farmer journey:
- diagnosis apps identify crop problems;
- satellite tools monitor fields;
- farm-management systems record operations;
- fintech products provide credit;
- extension platforms distribute advisory messages.

Smallholder farmers and field programmes still have to connect these decisions manually.

### The AgriDome difference

AgriDome is designed around one operational loop:

**farmer observation → risk detection → prioritised action → follow-up record → evidence**

The platform combines:
- offline farm records;
- climate and greenhouse guidance;
- crop-stage management;
- AI crop-health triage;
- yield and revenue planning;
- local-language farmer advice;
- de-identified pilot/evidence exports;
- programme coordinator analysis.

### Competitive position

A September 2026 feature-fit benchmark compared AgriDome with Plantix, PlantVillage Nuru, OneSoil, Cropwise, AGRIVI, farmOS, DigiFarm, eProd and FAO's Digital Services Portfolio.

AgriDome scored **89.8/100** for the specific target segment of **African smallholder greenhouse/intensive-vegetable programmes**, ahead of FAO DSP at 76.0 and Cropwise at 69.8.

This is a segment-fit benchmark, not a claim that AgriDome is more mature or globally superior to those products.

### Why partners should care

For an NGO, university, extension service, cooperative or funder, AgriDome can become both:
1. the farmer's daily decision-support tool; and
2. the programme's field-evidence layer.

That reduces the gap between "we delivered an advisory intervention" and "we can show what farmers recorded, did and achieved."

### Next partnership-ready capabilities
